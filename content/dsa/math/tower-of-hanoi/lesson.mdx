# Tower Of Hanoi

## Hook

Move a tower through an auxiliary shelf while never putting a large plate on a smaller plate.

## Watch

Make room for the largest disk. Edit the validated input or seeded preset to explore a different case.

## How it works

1. Move n−1 disks from source to auxiliary.
2. Move the largest disk to the target.
3. Move n−1 disks from auxiliary to target.

## Complexity

The optimal canonical three-peg plan has exactly 2ⁿ−1 moves and O(n) recursion depth. The interactive trace is capped at eight disks. The chart names its counted or modeled work rather than pretending animation duration is a benchmark.

## Code

## When to use

Use it to understand recursive decomposition, ordering constraints and exponential output size.

## Interview pitfalls

A legal board must keep larger disks below smaller ones. Even an efficient generator cannot avoid the exponentially many required moves.

# Reservoir Sampling

## Hook

Keep a small display shelf while boxes keep arriving, giving every box the same eventual chance to stay.

## Watch

A fair sample from a stream of unknown length. Edit the validated input or seeded preset to explore a different case.

## How it works

1. Fill the first k slots.
2. For item i, draw uniformly from indices zero through i.
3. Replace reservoir slot j only if j&lt;k.

## Try it

Predict the next arithmetic decision, cache victim, sample replacement or cluster assignment before revealing it.

## Complexity

One pass takes O(n) time and O(k) space. At step i, the new item enters with probability k/(i+1), preserving every item’s equal inclusion probability. The chart names its counted or modeled work rather than pretending animation duration is a benchmark.

## When to use

Use it when data arrives as a stream and the total length cannot be stored or known in advance.

## Interview pitfalls

Sampling positions is different from deduplicating equal values. The seeded generator is reproducible for teaching, not cryptographically secure randomness.

# Pagerank

## Hook

A random surfer follows links most of the time and occasionally jumps to a random page.

## Watch

Follow links without losing probability. Edit the validated input or seeded preset to explore a different case.

## How it works

1. Start with uniform probability.
2. Reserve teleportation weight for every vertex.
3. Redistribute rank from pages without outgoing links.
4. Distribute each linked page’s remaining weight over its outgoing links.

## Complexity

I sparse power iterations cost O(I(V+E)) time and O(V+E) space. Damping below one helps the random-surfer distribution converge. The chart names its counted or modeled work rather than pretending animation duration is a benchmark.

## When to use

Use it to study link-based importance and random walks on directed networks.

## Interview pitfalls

Dangling rank must be redistributed instead of deleted. Rank is a network statistic, not a measure of factual truth or personal worth.

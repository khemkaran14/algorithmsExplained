### AWS and GCP comparison

AWS: SQS work queues / SNS fan-out concepts. GCP: Pub/Sub subscription concepts. These services have different ordering, acknowledgment, retention and optional delivery guarantees. The atomic inbox protects a modeled local effect; external email/charges still need their own idempotency protocol. Do not infer universal exactly-once delivery from this diagram.

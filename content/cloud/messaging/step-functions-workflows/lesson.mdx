# Step Functions Workflows

## Hook

An orchestrator records progress, but an unknown external outcome still needs reconciliation.

## Watch

Choose AWS or GCP mapping, change one validated model parameter and load the trace. Provider labels identify concepts; the model never provisions resources or calls a provider account.

## How it works

Use an order saga as a worked workflow: reserve inventory, authorize a hold, reserve a courier, capture, fulfill. Compensate known failures in reverse order; retain resources after an uncertain capture until an explicit reconciliation result.

### AWS and GCP comparison

AWS: Step Functions orchestration concepts. GCP: Workflows orchestration concepts. The trace is a business-workflow model, not an ASL or Google Workflows deployment. Production needs durable orchestration state, provider-specific retries/catches, scoped IAM, task idempotency and reconciliation. Compensation may itself fail and require further recovery.
